about me

My name is Marc. I'm a creative professional and software engineer whose background bridges media and entertainment with software development.

  1. 2006 — 2010

    Film school

    I graduated from DeSales University in Center Valley, PA, in 2010, with a major in Television/Film and a minor in Business.

  2. 2011 — 2019

    Editing & color

    Early in my career I spent two years as the lead editor of special programming for Reelz Channel. After that I freelanced for online media outlets and brands such as Reddit, Pop Sci, and The Verge. I also worked as a colorist for feature films, short-form content, and dailies.

  3. 2010s

    Outdoors

    My career took a turn toward the outdoor industry. I started The Crux Collective, a short-lived outdoor news aggregator, and later founded Rove Media, a one-man media consultancy. Through Rove Media I made videos and materials for brands like L.L.Bean, Outside Online, Patrol USA, and GoPro.

  4. 2017 — 2024

    Cinnafilm

    I was a Senior Software Developer at Cinnafilm, working on PixelStrings, a cloud-based video processing platform. I designed and built the API and backend, orchestrated the cloud infrastructure, and maintained a horizontally scalable video transcoding system, mostly in Python, JavaScript, and TypeScript with Flask, Django, and FastAPI.

  5. 2024 — now

    Mux

    Since Q3 of 2024 I've been a Software Developer at Mux, on the Ingest and Processing team.

  6. on the side

    Projects

    I made LineDream, a Python generative art library built for SVG output and originally meant for pen plotter art. I'm also building Asset Veranda, media asset management software, and TimecodeTool, a timecode CLI.

  7. home

    Bozeman

    These days I'm enjoying the fruits of living in Bozeman, Montana, where I ski, mountain bike, and hike.
